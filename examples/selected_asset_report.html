05 / METHODOLOGY & ROBUSTNESS

How to read this report

Risk = 100 × (T × V × R × C / 10,000)¼

All four pillars range from 0 to 10. Threat combines exposed warning channels. Vulnerability weights audit condition, control gaps and irreplaceability at 40%, 30% and 30%. Recovery gap is ten times one minus the mean of six recovery facts. Consequence uses the maximum category, supply-share and population-share score.

Published scores are rounded to whole points. Low: 0–19; Guarded: 20–39; Elevated: 40–59; High: 60–79; Severe: 80–100. Category 1–2 assets with T, V or R at least 9 receive a minimum High band. Confidence: A at least 90%, B at least 75%, C at least 50%, otherwise D of required inputs current.

Assumptions and limitations

  • City and asset data are fictional. The dashboard’s 3D models are illustrative; this report uses a vector plan view for legible printing.
  • History uses the supplied static baseline, warning versions and explicitly dated input updates. It cannot reconstruct unrecorded changes. One latest score per recorded timestamp is shown, with repeat runs collapsed.
  • Unknown audits and controls receive conservative scores. Unknown recovery facts earn no recovery credit. Evidence freshness and channel coverage affect confidence separately.
  • Full exercises are treated as current because no exercise date is supplied. Driver effects are separate counterfactuals; intervention benefits are recomputed jointly.
  • Some reference pillar displays differ by 0.01 at rounding boundaries. This report preserves engine outputs.
  • Plan assumptions require human verification. A proposed check-up is a recommendation for analyst review.
  • The saved HTML includes a complete structured evidence snapshot. Use Download evidence JSON for machine-readable values, including every current score record and all dated chart points. A printed PDF contains the visible report, not the embedded JSON attachment.
